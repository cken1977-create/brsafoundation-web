Privacy Policy

Your privacy, plainly stated.

What we collect through this website, how we use it, and how to reach us with privacy questions.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how BRSA Foundation Inc. ("the Foundation," "we," "us"), a 501(c)(3) public charity located at 5400 Gibson Blvd SE, Bldg 11, Albuquerque, NM 87108, collects and uses information through this website, www.brsafoundation.org.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form or pledge form, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, and message, and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number, organization, topic, and pledge amount. We do not ask for, and you should not send, card numbers, bank account numbers, or Social Security numbers through these forms.

Gifts by mail. If you mail a check, we keep the information needed to record your gift and send you an acknowledgment, such as your name, address, gift amount, and date.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the date and time. This is used to deliver and secure the site.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. We do not set cookies to track you. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and animation scripts from the jsDelivr content network, and videos are served from our hosting provider's storage. Those providers receive your IP address and browser information when they deliver these files to you, under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To reply to your message or pledge and to follow up about partnerships or gifts you asked about.
  • To process, record, and acknowledge donations and to keep the records a charity is required to keep.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect this website.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not add you to a mailing list from a form submission unless you ask us to.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or donor information to anyone. We share information only with service providers who help us run this website and store form submissions (such as our website hosting and database providers), who may use it only to provide those services to us, or when required by law.

Data retention and security

We keep form submissions as long as needed to respond and to manage the relationship you asked about, and we keep donation records as long as the law requires. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access controls, to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information you have sent us, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@brsafoundation.org or calling (505) 750-9202. We will honor your request unless we must keep certain records by law.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through it.

Links to other websites

Our site links to other websites, such as our technology partner. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
