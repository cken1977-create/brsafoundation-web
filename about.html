About the Foundation

Closing the access gap, not lowering the standard.

The BRSA Foundation is a technology-based 501(c)(3) public charity based in Albuquerque, New Mexico. We fund access to behavioral readiness evaluation, certification, and longitudinal readiness support for individuals who face financial barriers to it.

Home / About
Our Mission

Why the Foundation exists.

The BRSA Foundation exists to fund access to behavioral readiness evaluation and certification so individuals can demonstrate capability within housing, workforce, reentry, and education systems.

Our role is not to weaken the standard. Our role is to make access to the standard possible.
Who we are

A public charity built for accountable outcomes.

People are often denied opportunity not because they lack potential, but because there is no credible system capable of verifying their readiness. The Foundation pays for access to a governed, standards-based system that does, and that tracks outcomes over time.
